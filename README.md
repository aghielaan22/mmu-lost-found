## 📖 About The Project

Misplacing personal belongings—student IDs, laptops, tumblers, or books—is a common hassle on campus. **MMU Lost & Found** is built to bridge the gap between finders and owners. 

This platform enables students and staff to quickly log lost items, report items they've found, and browse active listings to reunite belongings with their rightful owners with minimal friction.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* **Report Lost Items:** Submit details including item description, category, last known location, and date/time.
* **Log Found Items:** Found something around campus? Post it with details to help the owner track it down.
* **Search & Filter:** Browse active listings filtered by category, building, or date.
* **Claim Verification Workflow:** Secure methods to verify ownership before handoff.
* **Responsive Layout:** Optimized for use on mobile phones, tablets, and desktop browsers.

## 💡 Usage

* **For Owners:** Navigate to the platform, browse the "Found Items" board, or submit a new "Lost Item" ticket.
* **For Finders:** Snap a quick photo, fill out the "Found Item" form with the location, and publish it to the community board.
